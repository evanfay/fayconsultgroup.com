Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Fay Consult Group ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through fayconsultgroup.com and our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you fill out a form, book a call, or email us, we collect what you provide, such as your name, business name, phone number, email address, website, and details about your business.
  • Website usage. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and which buttons are clicked. This uses cookies and similar technology.
  • Client lead data. When a business uses our speed-to-lead service, we process information about that business's leads and customers (such as names, phone numbers, and messages) on the business's behalf.

How we use information

  • To respond to your request, including running a free response-time test you ask for
  • To contact you by phone, email, or (if you opt in) text message about your request
  • To provide, run, and improve our services
  • To understand and improve how our website works
  • To meet legal obligations

Text messaging (SMS)

If you opt in to text messages, we use your mobile number to send messages related to your request or our services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except service providers who help us deliver messages.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as form handling, scheduling, analytics, email, and messaging providers, and only as needed for them to provide those services. We may also share information if required by law.

Client data

When we run speed-to-lead or other services for a business, that business owns its lead and customer data. We use it only to provide the service, and we will export or delete it at the business's request, subject to legal requirements.

Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

  • Opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP.
  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing us.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
