About

Hi, I'm Evan.

I started Fay Consult Group to help local businesses stop losing jobs to slow follow-up.

Evan Fay

Founder · Phoenixville, PA

I live in Phoenixville, in Chester County. My background is in supply chain and operations. I studied at Penn State's Smeal College of Business, then worked in freight logistics and in production scheduling at a manufacturer.

That work taught me the same lesson over and over: delays in handoffs cost money. A truck waits on paperwork. A job waits on a part. A customer waits on a callback. Fix the handoff, and the results follow.

Speed to lead is that lesson applied to your front door. When a customer reaches out, the gap between their inquiry and your reply is where a lot of jobs are won or lost. I build systems that close that gap, then measure it, so you can see what's working.

I work with every client directly. No account managers, no hype, and no software for you to learn.
