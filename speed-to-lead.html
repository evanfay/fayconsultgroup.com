The questions owners ask first

It works for you, not the other way around

"Will it sound like a robot?"

No. Messages are short, friendly, and written in your voice. We write them together during setup, and you approve every one before it goes out.

"What if it says something wrong?"

It only answers from information you approve: your services, hours, service area, and pricing rules. Anything else goes to a person. It won't quote prices unless you set the rules.

"Do I need new software?"

No. It works alongside what you already use, like your phone, your calendar, and your email. If you use a CRM or job management tool, tell us which one and we'll show you how it connects.

"Do I keep my phone number?"

Yes. Missed-call text-back works through simple call forwarding or a tracking number. Your customers keep calling the same number they always have.

"Who owns the data?"

You do. Your leads, contacts, and conversation history belong to your business. We'll export everything for you on request. No lock-in.

"Can I jump in?"

Any time. You or your team can take over any conversation with one tap, and the automation steps aside.

Compliance & trust

Set up to follow the rules

Texting and calling customers comes with rules. We build them in from day one.

  • Consent first. The system only contacts people who reached out to you. Your forms get clear opt-in language.
  • STOP means stop. Opt-outs are honored instantly, and do-not-contact requests are respected across every channel.
  • Business texting registration handled. We take care of carrier registration (A2P 10DLC) so your texts get delivered.
  • AI is always disclosed. Any AI voice or chat introduces itself up front, like "This is the virtual assistant for your business." No deceptive tactics.
  • Call recording notices. Pennsylvania is a two-party consent state, so calls that are recorded include a notice where required.

This describes how we set things up. It's general information, not legal advice. Your attorney can advise on the rules for your specific business.

FAQ

Straight answers

How fast is "instant"?

Usually within seconds. The goal is a personal reply in under 60 seconds, any time of day, every day of the week. Most leads hear back before they've had time to contact anyone else.

Does it replace my office staff or salespeople?

No. It covers the gap between when a lead reaches out and when your team can respond. It handles the first reply, the basic questions, and the follow-up, then hands a warm lead to your people. Your team spends its time on conversations that are ready to go.

Will customers know it's automated?

Text messages are written in your voice and come from your business. Any AI voice or chat always introduces itself as a virtual assistant. We never pretend an AI is a person. In practice, customers care most about getting a fast, helpful answer.

What happens after hours and on weekends?

Leads still get an instant reply, with after-hours messaging you choose. They can answer a few questions and book a time, and you get the summary when you're ready. Urgent leads can be flagged or escalated, based on your rules.

Do I need new software or a new phone number?

No. You keep your number, and we work with the calendar and tools you already use. We host and manage the system, so there's nothing new for you to learn.

What about leads I already have?

We can run a reactivation campaign to old leads and past quotes that never got a yes or no. It's often the fastest way to find a few jobs you already paid to get.

How do you handle texting laws and opt-outs?

The system only contacts people who reached out to your business, your forms get clear opt-in language, and anyone who replies STOP is removed immediately. We handle business texting registration with the carriers. This is how we set things up, not legal advice.

What if I get a lot of junk leads?

Spam and junk leads are filtered out, and the qualification questions sort real customers from tire-kickers. You only get alerts for leads worth your time.

Can I cancel? Who owns the data?

You own your leads, contacts, and conversation history, and we'll export them for you on request. We'll walk through the exact terms with your quote. There's no lock-in on your data.

How soon will I see results?

Most setups go live in about a week. Faster replies start working on the first new lead after that. We review the numbers with you weekly for the first 30 days, so you can see exactly what's happening.
