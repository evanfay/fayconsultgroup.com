The problem

Leads don't wait. They call the next business.

Your customers usually reach out to two or three businesses at once. The one that gets back to them first has the best shot at the job.

Speed wins the job

Industry studies consistently show that response time is one of the biggest factors in whether a lead turns into a customer. The odds drop fast after the first few minutes.

Hours, not minutes

Most small businesses reply to new inquiries hours later, or the next day. By then, the customer has often called someone else.

The biggest leaks

Missed calls while you're on a job. Web forms that come in after hours. Weekend inquiries that sit until Monday morning.
